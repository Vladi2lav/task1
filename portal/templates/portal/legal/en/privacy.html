Privacy and personal data processing policy

1. General provisions

1.1. This Policy sets out how personal data is collected, processed and protected when using the website “ETS and Caspy Exchange Trading Results” (the “Portal”). It has been drawn up in accordance with Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 94-V of 21 May 2013 “On Personal Data and Their Protection” (the “Law”).

1.2. The owner and operator of the Portal’s database is the site owner: {{ site_owner }} (the “Operator”). Contact address: {{ contact_email }}.

1.3. The Portal is a non-commercial information project. The Operator processes personal data to the minimum extent necessary and only for the purposes set out in section 3.

2. What data is processed

2.1. Visitor data

The Portal does not require registration, does not create visitor accounts and has no forms for entering a visitor’s name, phone number, email address or other details.

When the Portal is accessed, the server and the hosting provider automatically record technical data in their logs: IP address, date and time of the request, address of the requested page, response code, browser details (User-Agent) and the address of the page from which the visitor came. This data is not used to identify the visitor and is not combined with other information.

The Portal uses only necessary cookies and a consent record in the browser storage — see the Cookie Policy. Web analytics tools and advertising or tracking services are not used on the Portal.

If a visitor writes to the Operator by email, the Operator processes the sender’s address and the content of the message in order to reply.

2.2. Information in trading results

The exchange trading results that the exchanges publish openly include the names and identification numbers (BIN, and for individual entrepreneurs also IIN) of buyers, sellers and brokers. The name of an individual entrepreneur may contain their surname, first name and patronymic; such information is personal data.

The basis for processing this information is its prior lawful publication by the exchanges in publicly available form as part of the exchange trading results. The Portal reproduces the information to the extent published by the original source, does not supplement it with other data about the data subject, does not build profiles of individuals and uses the information solely for non-commercial information purposes.

3. Purposes of processing

  • ensuring the operation of the Portal and its technical security (protection against request forgery, detection of failures and attacks);
  • providing users with information on exchange trading results, search and export;
  • handling requests and demands of personal data subjects.

4. Retention periods

  • server logs — no longer than necessary to ensure the operation and security of the Portal, and within the log retention periods set by the hosting provider;
  • cookies — for the periods stated in the Cookie Policy;
  • information in trading results — for as long as the Portal exists or until a data subject’s demand for its deletion is satisfied;
  • correspondence with data subjects — no more than one year after the request has been dealt with.

When the retention period expires or the purpose of processing has been achieved, the data is destroyed or anonymised.

5. Disclosure to third parties

5.1. The Operator does not sell personal data and does not transfer it to third parties for advertising or marketing.

5.2. The Portal is hosted by a hosting provider: {{ hosting_provider }}. The hosting provider processes technical data (request logs) in the course of providing hosting services. If the hosting provider’s servers are located outside the Republic of Kazakhstan, technical data is transferred in compliance with the requirements of the Law on cross-border transfer of personal data.

5.3. Data may be provided to government authorities in the cases and in the manner provided for by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

5.4. The Portal contains links to the exchanges’ websites. When you follow them, data is processed by the owners of those websites in accordance with their own policies.

6. Data protection

The Operator takes the necessary legal, organisational and technical measures to protect personal data: it minimises the amount of data collected, does not create visitor accounts, restricts access to the administrative section and the database, and uses protection against cross-site request forgery (CSRF).

7. Rights of the personal data subject

In accordance with the Law, the personal data subject has the right to:

  • know whether the Operator holds their personal data and receive information about its processing;
  • demand that their personal data be changed or supplemented where there are documented grounds;
  • demand that their personal data be blocked and destroyed if its processing does not comply with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
  • withdraw consent to processing if the processing was based on consent;
  • appeal against the actions (or inaction) of the Operator to the authorised body for personal data protection or to a court;
  • exercise other rights provided for by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

8. How to send a request or a deletion demand

8.1. Requests are sent to {{ contact_email }}. A request should include:

  • the applicant’s surname and first name (or company name) and contact details for the reply;
  • which information the request concerns (participant name, exchange, trading date or a link to a page of the Portal);
  • the substance of the demand: to provide information, correct, block or delete information.

8.2. To protect against unlawful requests, the Operator may ask for confirmation that the applicant is the personal data subject or their representative.

8.3. The Operator considers the request and reports the results within the time limits established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan. A satisfied demand is fulfilled for the Portal’s data; the information on the exchange’s website is not changed — questions about its publication should be addressed to the respective exchange.

9. Changes to the Policy

The Operator may change this Policy. A new version takes effect when it is published on this page. The date of the current version is given at the end of the document.
