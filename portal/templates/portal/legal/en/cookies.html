Cookie policy

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in the visitor’s browser and receives back with subsequent requests. Besides cookies, a website may store data in the browser’s local storage (localStorage); such data stays on the device and is not sent to the server automatically.

2. Which cookies the Portal uses

The Portal uses only necessary (technical and functional) first-party cookies. Without them, form protection, signing in to the administrative section and remembering the selected interface language do not work.

3. What the Portal does not have

  • analytics and statistics cookies (visit counters, web analytics systems);
  • advertising and tracking cookies, including third-party cookies;
  • embedded external scripts, fonts and widgets: the chart library is loaded from the Portal’s server.

4. How to manage cookies

The visitor can delete cookies and site data or block them in the browser settings. Refusing necessary cookies does not prevent viewing tables and news, but may make sending forms (for example, a manual collection run), signing in to the administrative section and remembering the chosen language unavailable.

By clicking “Accept” in the notice, the visitor confirms that they have read this Policy. Since the Portal uses only necessary cookies, their operation does not depend on this note.
